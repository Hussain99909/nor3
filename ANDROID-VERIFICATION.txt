Runtime device testing
- Physical Android phones have not been tested directly.
- Installation on an Android 16.1 x86_64 emulator: PASS (adb Success).
- Launch on that emulator: FAILED before Unity gameplay startup.
  The native crash trace is in translated ARM64 linker/ZIP loading
  (/memfd:exec and /system/bin/arm64/linker64, called from libmain.so).
- This suggests a native-loader/emulation compatibility issue, but
  does not prove the APK will work on a physical phone. Native phone
  runtime, appearance, gestures and vibration remain unverified.
- Unity documents Android emulators as unsupported:
  https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/platform-specific/android/introducing/requirements-and-compatibility
- Treat this APK as a test build, not a device-validated release.
